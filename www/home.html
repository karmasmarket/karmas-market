About Karmas Market

Who We Are

Karmas Market is a Nigerian digital marketplace built to empower entrepreneurs, freelancers, creators, and businesses to buy, sell, and grow online. We provide a platform where digital products, services, apps, and creative tools come together in one place.


Our Mission

To make digital commerce accessible, affordable, and secure for every Nigerian — from solo freelancers to growing businesses.


What We Offer

• Products — Buy and sell digital goods
• Services — Hire and offer freelance services
• Apps — Discover and distribute apps
• Creator Tools — Design flyers, ads and videos with Karma Ad Maker

Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026


Digital Products

All sales are final once a product has been downloaded or accessed. Refunds are only issued where the product is proven to be significantly different from its description.


Services / Freelancing

Funds are held in escrow until the buyer confirms delivery. If a seller fails to deliver, the buyer may raise a dispute and receive a full refund.


How to Request a Refund

Contact support@karmasmarket.ng within 7 days of purchase with your order details.


Approved Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to your Karmas Wallet within 5-7 business days.
